# 📹 Easy Video Creation Guide for DCSA

## You Don't Need Fancy Equipment!

Your smartphone is all you need to create authentic, engaging videos that resonate with your audience.

---

## 🎥 Video Ideas (Pick One to Start)

### 1. **Debt Myths Busted** (2-3 minutes each)
- "Will debt review ruin my life?" (Spoiler: No!)
- "Can I still buy groceries in debt review?" (Yes!)
- "What if I lose my job during debt review?" (We've got answers)

### 2. **Real Client Stories** (3-5 minutes)
- Interview past clients (faces blurred if needed)
- Focus on emotions: "How did it feel before?" "What changed?"
- Keep it real and authentic

### 3. **Behind the Scenes** (1-2 minutes)
- Office tour: "Where the magic happens"
- Meet the team: Quick intros with fun facts
- Day in the life of a debt counsellor

### 4. **Process Walkthrough** (3-4 minutes)
- "What Actually Happens in Debt Review"
- Screen record + voiceover showing the portal
- Make it visual and easy to follow

## 📱 How to Record (Super Simple)

### Equipment Needed:
- ✅ Your smartphone
- ✅ Natural light (face a window)
- ✅ Quiet room
- ✅ That's it!

### Recording Tips:
1. **Hold phone horizontally** (landscape mode)
2. **Face a window** for natural lighting
3. **Stand/sit 1-2 meters** from camera
4. **Use a chair** to prop up phone at eye level
5. **Record 3-5 takes** - pick the best one

### What to Wear:
- Smart casual (like you're meeting a client)
- Avoid busy patterns or stripes
- DCSA colors are great: Navy, teal, or neutral

## ✂️ Free Editing Tools

### Mobile Editing:
- **CapCut** (iOS/Android) - FREE, easy, professional
- **InShot** (iOS/Android) - FREE with watermark

### Desktop Editing:
- **DaVinci Resolve** - FREE, professional grade
- **Shotcut** - FREE, simple

### Quick Edit Checklist:
1. Trim the start/end (remove "umm" moments)
2. Add text overlay with your name/title
3. Add DCSA logo in corner (optional)
4. Export as MP4, 1080p

## 📝 Sample Script Template

### Intro (10 seconds)
"Howzit! I'm [Name] from DCSA, and today I'm answering the question..."

### Main Content (90-120 seconds)
- Break into 3 points
- Use stories or examples
- Keep it conversational

### Outro (10 seconds)
"Questions? Hit that link below or WhatsApp us at 066 193 7596. Cheers!"

---

## 🎯 Video Best Practices

### DO:
✅ Speak naturally (like chatting with a friend)
✅ Smile and make eye contact with camera
✅ Use SA slang - be yourself!
✅ Keep it under 5 minutes
✅ Add subtitles (for silent viewing)

### DON'T:
❌ Read from a script robotically
❌ Use complex financial jargon
❌ Make it too long (attention spans!)
❌ Apologize for being on camera
❌ Over-edit (authentic > perfect)

## 📤 Where to Post Videos

1. **YouTube** (create DCSA channel)
   - Upload as unlisted first
   - Test link, then make public
   - Add to website

2. **Facebook** (DCSA page)
   - Native uploads get more reach
   - Pin important videos

3. **Instagram**
   - Reels (60 seconds max)
   - Cut longer videos into snippets

4. **TikTok**
   - Short format (30-60 seconds)
   - Trending sounds + debt tips
   - HUGE potential reach for 24-35 age group

5. **Website** (embed YouTube links)
   - Homepage video testimonials section
   - Blog posts with video

## 🚀 Quick Start Action Plan

### Week 1: Test Run
- Record a 60-second intro: "Hi, I'm [Name] from DCSA..."
- Practice editing with CapCut
- Show 2-3 friends for feedback

### Week 2: First Real Video
- Pick ONE myth to bust
- Record 3 takes
- Edit and upload to YouTube unlisted
- Share with team for feedback

### Week 3: Go Live
- Make video public
- Post to Facebook
- Add to website
- Celebrate! 🎉
